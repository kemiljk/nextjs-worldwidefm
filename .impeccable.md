## Design Context

### Users

Worldwide FM hosts and programme makers use the site to submit show details and large audio files for review and broadcast. They may have waited several minutes for an upload, so completion states must remove doubt about whether the file arrived and make the next action unmistakable.

### Brand Personality

Warm, candid, and culture-first. The interface should feel human and editorial rather than corporate or technical. In stressful moments it should be calm, direct, and honest without exposing infrastructure details.

### Aesthetic Direction

Follow Worldwide FM's established high-contrast editorial system: almost-black and warm white foundations, expressive display typography, compact monospaced utility text, and the existing bright accent palette. Prefer strong hierarchy and flat, purposeful surfaces over generic cards, gradients, or decorative effects. Support both existing light and dark contexts.

### Design Principles

1. Confirm exactly what the system received before describing a submission as successful.
2. Keep vendor names, response payloads, and recovery mechanics away from hosts unless they need to act on them.
3. In failure states, lead with what is safe, then state what happens next and whether the host must do anything.
4. Use one clear status treatment rather than stacking competing alerts and toasts.
5. Preserve the site's sharp editorial character while making operational messages warm and reassuring.
